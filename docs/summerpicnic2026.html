☀️ Chinmaya Ramalaya Summer Family Picnic 2026

Sunshine • Smiles • Seva • Togetherness

Join the Chinmaya Ramalaya family for one of our most anticipated community events of the year! Spend a relaxing summer afternoon enjoying great food, exciting games, outdoor fun, and meaningful fellowship with devotees, Bala Vihar families, youth, and friends.

Whether you're reconnecting with familiar faces, meeting new families, cheering on your team during friendly competitions, or simply enjoying quality time with your loved ones, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

🍔 Delicious Lunch 🎯 Fun Games 🎈 Kids Activities 🏆 Team Challenges 🎵 Music & Fun 📸 Family Memories

Bring your picnic spirit, your favorite smile, and your whole family as we celebrate friendship, community, and the joy of being together.

Registration is required to help us plan food and activities. We look forward to celebrating a memorable summer day with you!

"To give maximum happiness to the maximum number for the maximum time"
