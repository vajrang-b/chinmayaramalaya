☀️ Chinmaya Ramalaya Summer Family Picnic 2026

Sunshine • Smiles • Seva • Togetherness

Community Fellowship & Outdoor Fun

Join the Chinmaya Ramalaya family for one of our most anticipated community events of the year! Spend a relaxing summer afternoon enjoying delicious food, exciting games, outdoor fun, and meaningful fellowship with devotees, Bala Vihar families, youth, and friends.

Whether you're reconnecting with familiar faces, meeting new families, cheering on your team during friendly competitions, or simply enjoying quality time with your loved ones, there's something for everyone to enjoy.

🍔 Delicious Lunch
🎯 Fun Games
🎈 Kids Activities
🏆 Team Challenges
