About Chinmaya Ramalaya

Serving the Harleysville & Greater Philadelphia Community through Vedantic Wisdom, Bala Vihar, & Devotion

🌟 Our Motto & Vision

"To give maximum happiness to the maximum number for the maximum time"

Chinmaya Ramalaya in Harleysville, PA is dedicated to providing individuals and families from all backgrounds the timeless wisdom of Vedanta, practical spiritual growth, and a nurturing environment rooted in Sanatana Dharma.

🛕 Welcome to Chinmaya Ramalaya

Located at 991 Lucon Road, Harleysville, PA 19438, Chinmaya Ramalaya is the spiritual home for families across Montgomery, Bucks, and Greater Philadelphia counties.

Our center provides a peaceful sanctuary for regular temple worship, monthly ShriRama Parivar Abhishekam, Sudarshana Homam, Hanuman Pooja, adult Vedanta study groups, yoga sessions, and cultural celebrations.

We are dedicated to building a vibrant, close-knit spiritual community where adults, youth, and children grow together in knowledge, devotion, and selfless service.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦 Bala Vihar & Youth Programs

Our flagship Bala Vihar® program conducts weekly Sunday classes for children from pre-K through high school. Designed by Pujya Swami Chinmayananda, the curriculum instills noble values, cultural pride, and moral courage.

Alongside Bala Vihar, Chinmaya Ramalaya offers youth activities, Gita Chanting competitions, festival performances, and community service projects that empower our next generation.

Classes are led by dedicated trained Sevaks and Sevikas who strive to create a warm, engaging, and inspiring environment for every student.
