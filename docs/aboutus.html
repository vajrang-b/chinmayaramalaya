About Chinmaya Mission

Vedantic Wisdom, Spiritual Growth, and Community Service Across the World

🌟 Our Motto & Vision

"To give maximum happiness to the maximum number for the maximum time"

Our mission is to provide to individuals from any background, the wisdom of Vedanta and the practical means for spiritual growth and happiness, enabling them to become positive contributors to society.

🕉️ History & Teacher-Taught Tradition

Following the ancient Vedic teacher-taught tradition (guru-shishya parampara), Chinmaya Mission makes available the ageless wisdom of Vedanta—the knowledge of the one supreme Reality—and provides the tools to realize that wisdom in daily life.

Chinmaya Mission was established in India in 1953 by devotees of Pujya Swami Chinmayananda. Under his guidance, these devotees formed the nucleus of a spiritual renaissance movement that now encompasses a wide range of spiritual, educational, and charitable activities ennobling thousands of lives.

Administered globally by Central Chinmaya Mission Trust (CCMT) in Mumbai, India, the Mission operates over 300 centers worldwide under the guidance of His Holiness Swami Swaroopananda, supported by Swami Tejomayananda and dedicated spiritual teachers.

🌎 Chinmaya Mission West (CMW)

Pujya Gurudev Swami Chinmayananda first visited the United States in 1965, conducting spiritual yajnas and retreats every summer. Inspired by his teachings, devotees formed Study Groups and Bala Vihars across North America.

In 1975, Chinmaya Mission® West (CMW) was incorporated to administer and oversee the growing activities across the US and Canada. Today, CMW coordinates over 40 centers, initiating national projects, retreats, publishing Vedantic literature, and managing the Krishnalaya ashram retreat center in Northern California.

Here at Chinmaya Ramalaya (Chinmaya Mission Harleysville), we serve local families in Greater Philadelphia through regular Bala Vihar sessions, Satsangs, Puja services, and community seva.

Core Pillars of Chinmaya Mission

📖

Vedanta & Study Groups

Adult study groups and Jnana Yajnas systematically exploring the Bhagavad Gita, Upanishads, and Prakarana Granthas.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Bala Vihar®

Weekly value-based spiritual education for children from pre-K to high school, nurturing character and Vedic culture.

🔥

Chinmaya Yuva Kendra (CHYK)

Youth empowerment, leadership development, and spiritual retreats designed for young adults aged 18 to 28.

🤝

Seva & Community Relief

Charitable programs, disaster relief, medical aid, and community service activities expressing selfless devotion.
