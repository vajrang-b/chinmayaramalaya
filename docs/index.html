What is Chinmaya Mission?

Founded by Swami Chinmayananda, Chinmaya Mission is a global spiritual movement rooted in the timeless wisdom of the Bhagavad Gita and Upanishads. For over seven decades, the Mission has inspired individuals and families to live with purpose, values, and compassion.

🌎

Global Presence

350+ Centers serving communities across the world in 30 countries through education, culture, spirituality, and service.

👨‍👩‍👧‍👦

Bala Vihar

Nurturing young minds with values, character, confidence, and spiritual wisdom rooted in Sanatana Dharma.

🧘

Yoga & Meditation

🤝

Seva & Leadership

Opportunities for community service, youth empowerment, and leadership development.

🙏 Continuing Gurudev's Vision

Following Swami Chinmayananda's Mahasamadhi in 1993, Chinmaya Mission continues to flourish under the guidance of Swami Swaroopananda, supported by Swami Tejomayananda and dedicated spiritual teachers serving across the globe.
