🌸 Sri Krishna Parijatham

"The Divine cannot be possessed — only experienced through surrender."

🧠Ego & Identity

Satyabhama represents the Jeevatma — shaped by ego, attachment, and the illusion of possession.

🪷Divine Grace

The Parijata tree symbolizes grace — not controlled, but received by purity of heart.

🙏Devotion

Rukmini reflects surrender — the calm acceptance of the Divine will.

🔱Transformation

The journey moves from ego → awareness → surrender → union with Paramatma.

👑 Guru Hanumantha Rao Yellamanchili

Guru Sri Hanumantha Rao Yellamanchili, fondly known as Kumar, is a disciple of Padma Bhushan Dr. Vempati Chinna Satyam.

His training began at Kuchipudi Kalakshetra and deepened at the Kuchipudi Art Academy in Chennai under “Master Garu”.

He has performed across India, USA, Canada, Europe, and Asia in major Kuchipudi productions.

🏫Current Role

Principal and Lead Instructor, Visakhapatnam branch of the Kuchipudi Art Academy.

🌍Global Teaching

Trains students across India and the United States through workshops and remote instruction.

🏆Recognition

Natya Visaradha • Natya Praveena • Satyabhama Best Teacher Award • CCRT Scholarship Mentorship
